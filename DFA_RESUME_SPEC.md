# Don't fuck around résumé specification

Reconciled on 2026-10-14 from the established September 2026 instructions. This is the default DFA standard for résumé preparation and delivery. Current explicit instructions for a particular application take precedence over the defaults below.

## Design and formatting

- Font: Noto Sans.
- Accent colour: orange #E06E00 for the name and section headings.
- Body colour: dark charcoal. #292929 was used in a prior implementation; an exact charcoal hex is not an established universal requirement.
- Header: centred, with a clear hierarchy for name, professional title and contact information.
- Layout: single column, with no tables.
- Use short paragraphs and focused bullets.
- Use readable, balanced spacing, with breathing room between sections and efficient use of the page.
- Avoid cramped text, excessive blank space and unnecessary decoration.
- Default length: two polished pages. Longer versions have been accepted for particular applications requiring detailed evidence; treat them as application-specific exceptions.
- No fixed font sizes, margins or line-spacing values were recovered as established requirements. Select these to meet readability and layout goals, rather than claiming invented values are part of the specification.

## Content and evidence

- Read the actual source documents and relevant confirmed context.
- Use evidence to support reasoning and the resulting wording.
- Reconcile dates, role titles, employment arrangements, metrics, technical ownership and credential status across the application files.
- Never invent experience, achievements, metrics, qualifications or credentials.
- Distinguish architecture/design, implementation, recommendations and approval authority accurately.
- Tailor to the opportunity using supported experience.
- Prioritize concrete achievements and business value; remove repetition and fluff.
- Include certification codes and full names where applicable, with accurate earned dates and status.
- Do not present expired credentials as current.
- Resolve uncertain claims or omit them from the final résumé. Do not leave “approximately,” “provisional” or “to be confirmed” in a final résumé.
- Keep related résumé, matrix and reference documents consistent; clearly identify superseded versions.

## Deliverables and filenames

- Deliver PDF only unless DOCX is explicitly requested.
- Provide two identical PDFs with different filename conventions:
  - A Windows/share-friendly name.
  - A lowercase, hyphenated Linux-friendly name.
- A DOCX requested for a specific task is an exception to the delivery default, not a permanent change to the DFA standard.
- Complete the authorized work and provide usable final deliverables. Identify concrete blockers honestly.

## Verification before delivery

- Render the final document and visually inspect every page.
- Check factual accuracy against the sources.
- Check typography, Noto Sans usage, orange accents and charcoal body text.
- Check pagination, hierarchy, spacing, bullet consistency and readability.
- Check for clipping, overlap, missing glyphs, awkward page breaks and large unused areas.
- Verify that both filename variants contain the identical final PDF.
- Do not describe a draft or unverified output as completed.

## Communication and sending

- Keep progress updates and final explanations concise and concrete.
- Do not send emails or messages without first showing the complete draft, recipients, subject and attachments, and receiving explicit approval to send that exact version.
- “Reply,” “follow up” and “go” are not approval to send. Any subsequent change requires renewed approval.

## Reconciliation history

- September 1, 2026: the original DFA design used burnt orange #C65E1E and orange name/section styling.
- September 17–28, 2026: the established design was refined to Noto Sans, orange #E06E00, charcoal text, a centred header, one column and no tables.
- September 28, 2026: the user explicitly corrected the black-and-white interpretation: “Black and white? Don't fuck around isn't black-and-white.”
- October 14, 2026: these requirements were recovered and reconciled. Generic black-and-white preferences must not be used to erase the DFA orange styling.
